F1 RACE WEEKEND SESSION ORDER

  1. 1

    Free Practice 1 (FP1) — Aero Rake & Setup Calibration

    60 minutes of uninterrupted track time dedicated to verifying downforce correlation, tire degradation rates on hard and medium compounds, and baseline balance adjustments.

  2. 2

    Sprint Qualifying (SQ1, SQ2, SQ3) — Rapid Knockout Shootout

    Condensed 44-minute shootout determining the starting order for Saturday's Sprint. SQ1 and SQ2 run strictly on mandatory medium tires, before SQ3 unlocks soft compound runs.

  3. 3

    Sprint Race (100km / ~19 Laps) — Flat-Out Points Dash

    A pure high-speed sprint race without mandatory tire changes. Top 8 finishers score official World Drivers' Championship points (8-7-6-5-4-3-2-1).

  4. 4

    Grand Prix Qualifying (Q1, Q2, Q3) — Pole Position Battle

    Full 60-minute qualifying session with maximum engine deployment mode to set the 20-car starting grid for Sunday's main event.

  5. 5

    The Grand Prix (305km / ~56 Laps) — Championship Main Event

    The headline race of the weekend. Drivers must use at least two different dry tire compounds under green flag conditions, competing for 25 points and the DHL Fastest Lap point.
